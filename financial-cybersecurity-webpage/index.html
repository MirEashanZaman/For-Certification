Tips for Financial Cybersecurity

Practical everyday habits to keep your bank accounts, cards, and personal financial data safe online.

1. Use Strong, Unique Passwords

Create a separate, complex password for every banking, payment, and investment account. Consider using a reputable password manager so you never have to reuse credentials across sites.

2. Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

Turn on two-factor or multi-factor authentication wherever available. Even if someone obtains your password, an extra verification step helps block unauthorized access.

3. Watch Out for Phishing Scams

Be cautious of unexpected emails, texts, or calls claiming urgent issues with your account. Always verify messages by visiting your bank's official website or calling the number on the back of your card.

4. Monitor Your Accounts Regularly

Review your bank and credit card statements frequently and enable instant transaction alerts so you can spot and report suspicious activity immediately.

5. Avoid Banking on Public Wi-Fi

Avoid logging into financial accounts over unsecured public networks. Use a trusted private connection, mobile data, or a reliable VPN when checking accounts away from home.

Stay alert and keep your devices and financial apps updated.
